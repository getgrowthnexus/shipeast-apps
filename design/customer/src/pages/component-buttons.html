Buttons

One button for the whole app: flat, pill-shaped, no gradients. A screen gets one filled pill and the rest of its affordances are words. From lib/widgets/se_button.dart.

Variants

primaryFlat action red #E30D3B, white label at 4.79:1, plus a restrained lift (glow). Never the identity red — white on #F72B54 fails AA
secondarybrandSoft #FFF0F4 fill, brandInk #B60C31 label. No lift
ghostTransparent, 1.5px ink200 border, ink700 label
destructivedangerSoft fill, deep-maroon label — visibly not the primary action
disabledred200 fill at 60% opacity, no shadow

Sizes

Label weight600 at every size — buttons sit on the "all emphasis below title" rung
Icon sizefont size + 4px, set 8px before the label
WidthFull-bleed by default; expand: false gives 20px side padding instead
Radiusfull. Buttons became pills in the 2026 restyle — next to a pill chip, a pill tab and a pill toggle, a chamfered button was the odd one out

Press & loading

PressScale 0.97 over 100ms plus a light haptic. The shadow drops on the way down
Reduced motionThe scale is skipped; the haptic is not
LoadingA 20px spinner replaces the label. The button keeps its width, so the layout does not jump when the request returns
