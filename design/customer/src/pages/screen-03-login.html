Spec

Why two surfacesThe previous auth screens centred an emblem, a title and the fields as one floating group, which read as a column of unrelated parts with dead air around them. Splitting the screen gives every element a job: the cap is brand and context, the sheet is the task
Cap heightA minimum of 28.5% of the viewport minus the status bar — the seam is measured from the top of the screen, which is where the eye measures it from. Sized to its own text it came out at a fifth of the screen, and the form then read as the page with the brand as a band stuck on top
Never clippedIt is a floor, not a fixed height: a wrapped title, a large system text size or a short phone all push straight past it
KeyboardThe cap collapses to a single line (title 22px, no subtitle) once the inset passes 120px. On a small phone a fixed hero would push the last field under the keyboard
Titledisplay at 30px / 26px / 22px for roomy, tight and keyboard states
Sheet fillsurface0, not surface50 — the auth sheet is the lighter of the two card tones because it is the whole page rather than a card on a page
GoogleFirebase's own OAuth flow, so no google_sign_in plugin and no native config churn
