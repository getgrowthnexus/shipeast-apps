Elevation — rose-black, never neutral

Over a blush ground a neutral shadow turns grey and fights the palette, so every shadow is cast in rose-black (110,26,48). Each level pairs a tight contact shadow with a wider soft pool, and the pool carries a negative spread so it stays under the card instead of haloing sideways.
