Privacy & security

Route /privacy-security. States plainly what is stored, then gives the two controls that matter: reset the password, delete everything.

Privacy & security
What we hold, and what you control

What we hold

Profile
Name, phone number, email address
Delivery addresses
The places you have saved
Order history
Your past and current orders
Ratings
The scores you give drivers and merchants

We never sell your data. It is used to run ShipEast and nothing else.

Security

We send a reset link to your registered email address; you set the new password there.

Delete account

Permanently deletes your ShipEast account and everything attached to it. This cannot be undone.

Spec

Inventory firstFour read-only rows naming what is held, in plain words. No carets — these rows go nowhere, they inform
Two panelsEach destructive-ish control gets its own panel with a sentence of explanation above the button. A bare "Delete my account" button in a row group would be far too easy to hit
DeletionBehind a confirm sheet, destructive variant, and it says cannot be undone before the sheet as well as in it
ResetSecondary, not primary. It is a safe action, and the screen's loudest control should not be one you press by accident
