Spec

Headlinehero(37) — weight 900, leading 1.05, tracking −0.028em. 37px is width × 0.09 clamped to 28–37, so two phones 8dp apart do not get headlines 6dp apart
Two tonesLine one shellInk, line two shellMark. The colour break is the emphasis; there is no second type size
Lockup26px wordmark with the legal name tucked under it. A delivery app asking for an address and a phone number should say who is actually collecting them
Body copybodyS at shellInk 78%, leading 1.5, capped at 330px so it breaks where it was designed to break
Pills13% white fill, 18% white hairline, 13px shellMark glyph
Rider band46% of the shell height, never leaving the copy under ~260dp. The copy scrolls if even that is not enough — a short scroll is a better failure than an overflow stripe
The sheetPadding is deliberately generous (40px top). It is the only white on the screen and it carries the only button; sized to its contents it reads as a strip pushed against the bottom edge rather than a surface the page comes to rest on
RhythmEvery vertical gap is multiplied by an air factor — 1.0, 0.6 or 0.35 by available height. A short phone gets the same composition, tighter
