Spec

Not shippingNothing crosses a border. The framing is "we shop here and deliver here", and the weight field that used to exist was replaced by a budget — you are buying goods, not posting a parcel
It is an enquiryThe action is Send request, not "Place order", and the glyph is a send arrow rather than a cart. Prices move with the store and the day, so the team confirms before anything is charged
Two blocks"Where we reach you" (the buyer, abroad) and "Who receives it in Jamaica" (the recipient). Two people, two panels — collapsing them into one form is how the wrong person gets called
Info panelAn infoSoft panel rather than a SeNotice: it is a paragraph of explanation, not a one-line advisory
ExclusionsStated up front, in a notice, before the form is filled in — not after submitting
