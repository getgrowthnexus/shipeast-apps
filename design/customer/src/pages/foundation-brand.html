The shell — a flat brand surface, and the quiet geometry on it

Every ember and sunset gradient was retired in the 2026 restyle. Depth now comes from rose-black shadows and hairline rings, never a sheen. Where a brand field needs relief it gets soft discs bleeding off the edges at 5–6% white — depth without a gradient. The single surviving gradient in the system is the ink scrim over photography, because a legibility scrim is a function rather than a decoration.
